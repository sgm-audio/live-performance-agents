# Social Media Branding & Marketing Strategy

## Brand Positioning

### Core Brand Identity

**Product Name:** **SonicMind Live** (powered by Live Performance Agents)

**Tagline:** *"Your Audience. Your Music. Your Control."*

**Mission:** Democratize live music performance technology while building authentic connections between artists and fans.

**Target Audience:**
1. **Primary:** Independent electronic music producers (18-35)
2. **Secondary:** Live-streaming DJs and beatmakers
3. **Tertiary:** Music educators and production streamers

## Platform-Specific Strategies

**Content Format:**
- 15-30 second clips
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Text overlays with key points

## Crisis Management & Brand Protection

### Reputation Monitoring

**Tools:**
- Google Alerts: "SonicMind Live"
- Social listening: Mention, Brandwatch
- Reddit monitoring: r/MusicProduction, r/Twitch
- Review platforms: Trustpilot, G2, Capterra

### Response Protocols

**Negative Review:**
1. Acknowledge within 24 hours
2. Apologize + offer solution
3. Take conversation to DM/email
4. Follow up publicly when resolved

**Technical Issue:**
1. Immediate status page update
2. Twitter/Discord announcement
3. Hourly updates until resolved
4. Post-mortem blog post
5. Goodwill gesture (free month, credits)

**Competitor Attacks:**
1. Monitor mentions
2. Respond factually, never emotional
3. Highlight unique value props
4. Let satisfied customers defend organically

## Metrics & KPIs

### Social Media Metrics (Track Weekly)

**Growth:**
- Follower count by platform
- Follower growth rate (% week-over-week)
- Reach and impressions

**Engagement:**
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + shares / followers)
- Comments per post
- Share rate
- Click-through rate (CTR)

**Conversion:**
- Website traffic from social
- Free trial signups (source tracked)
- Paid conversions attributed to social
- Revenue per follower

### Content Performance

**Top Performers:**
- Viral threshold: >100K views
- High engagement: >10% engagement rate
- High conversion: >5% CTR to website

**Bottom Performers:**
- Low engagement: <2% engagement rate
- Low reach: <1% of followers
- Action: Analyze and iterate format

## Launch Campaign (90 Days)

### Pre-Launch (Days -30 to 0)

**Week 1-2: Teaser Campaign**
- Social media: "Something is coming..."
- Email list building: Landing page with signup
- Influencer seeding: Send beta access to 10 key creators
- Content production: 30 pieces of launch content ready

**Week 3-4: Reveal**
- Product announcement video (YouTube)
- Feature showcase series (TikTok/Instagram)
- Press release to music tech blogs (MusicTech, DJ Mag)
- Waitlist opens (target: 5,000 signups)

### Launch Day (Day 0)

**Morning (9am EST):**
- Website goes live
- All social posts go out
- Email blast to waitlist
- Press release distribution

**Afternoon (12pm EST):**
- Reddit AMA (r/MusicProduction)
- Twitter Spaces live Q&A
- Influencer posts go live

**Evening (7pm EST):**
- Launch live stream (YouTube/Twitch)
- Live demo with Q&A
- Special launch pricing announcement

### Post-Launch (Days 1-90)

**Week 1:**
- Daily updates and user spotlights
- Address any technical issues publicly
- Collect feedback and testimonials

**Weeks 2-4:**
- Weekly feature deep-dives
- Case studies from early adopters
- Iterate based on feedback

**Months 2-3:**
- Shift to regular content schedule
- Launch first influencer partnerships
- Begin paid advertising
- Host first community challenge

**Goal:** 5,000 users, 500 paid subscribers by Day 90
